Open source · beta

Energy scheduling that refuses to lie to you.

VoltWise plans battery charging and EV sessions around dynamic tariffs, then audits its own plan for safety and stress-tests it against forecast uncertainty.

What it does

  • Dynamic-programming scheduler with a hard compute budget and ramp limits.
  • Solar curtailment handling and tariff-aware optimisation.
  • Post-optimisation safety audit — audit_plan() and stress_test().
  • A "never claim negative savings" rule so the UI can't overstate results.
  • Local web UI, hardware-free simulator and signed releases.
Beta, honestly. VoltWise is functionally simulated, not hardware-commissioned. Keep that framing until real users report inverter results.

Apache-2.0. Free alternatives (EMHASS, PredBat) are mature and capable — VoltWise's wedge is the safety audit and stress testing. The Pro Kit bundles documentation, screenshots and a signed licence key.
