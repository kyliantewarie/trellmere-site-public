Home Assistant / OpenWrt

Your TV doesn't need to call the manufacturer.

Smart Appliance Shield classifies traffic by appliance, blocks telemetry and keeps functional traffic working — without intercepting TLS or sending your data anywhere.

How it works

  • A SHA-256-signed ruleset with 5,238 patterns across 213 categories, verified at every start.
  • A DNS proxy and interceptor identify which appliance is making each request.
  • An enforcer blocks telemetry domains while allow-listing functional traffic.
  • A local dashboard shows what each device is trying to reach.
  • Ships as a Home Assistant add-on or a prebuilt OpenWrt .ipk.

What makes it different

Unlike a blanket Pi-hole-style blocklist, the classifier is per-appliance: the same domain can be allowed for your laptop and blocked for your TV. There is no TLS interception and no cloud component.

Verified. 166/166 security tests and 33/33 Gumroad-package checks pass. The prebuilt .ipk is structurally valid and boots with the signed ruleset.

Honest note: free alternatives (Pi-hole, AdGuard Home, Technitium, Cloudflare Zero Trust) handle general ad-blocking well. The value here is appliance-level classification with allow-listing. MIT licensed.
