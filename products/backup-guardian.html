Home Assistant add-on

A backup you haven't verified is just a hope.

Backup Guardian checks the age, size, coverage and integrity of your Home Assistant backups — including encrypted archives — and runs a restore drill. No restore to the live system, ever.

What it checks

  • Age — how old the newest good backup is.
  • Size — median-baseline drop detection to catch partial backups.
  • Coverage — are add-ons and config folders actually included?
  • Locations — every configured backup destination.
  • Members — archive contents verified against metadata.
  • Restore drill — extraction to a throwaway directory with path-traversal protection.
  • Encrypted backups — deliberately refuses to trust the metadata protected flag.

Readiness report

Turn any verification result into a branded PDF handover document — useful if you maintain Home Assistant for clients or just want a record you can print.

Fail-closed by design. 508/508 checks pass. The engine never writes to the live system and never claims a backup is good unless the evidence supports it.

MIT licensed. Free alternatives exist (BackupCheckup, ha-backup-tool) — you're paying for packaging, the report and support, not exclusive access to the checks.
