Blender add-on · v1.2

Sheet metal flat patterns, without leaving Blender.

Fold a part, unfold it correctly, and export a laser-ready DXF. SheetFab does the bend math properly — thickness, inside radius and K-factor — inside the app you already model in.

Stop round-tripping geometry through a second CAD program just to get a flat blank. SheetFab builds the part in a sidebar panel, develops it into a flat pattern with cut outline and bend lines, and writes out a layered DXF, a bend table and an SVG preview. Everything runs locally.

What you get

  • Build parts in-panel: material preset, thickness, inside radius, K-factor, leg lengths, bend angles, widths and holes. JSON remains available for power users.
  • Real bend allowance, bend deduction and outside-setback calculations — not a visual approximation.
  • Flat blank with cut outline, up/down bend lines and hole circles.
  • Layered DXF (CUT / BEND_UP / BEND_DOWN / HOLES / INFO), CSV bend table and SVG preview.
  • Shape-aware nesting and kerf compensation for sheet layouts.
  • 2D STEP import (POLYLINE, LINE, B_SPLINE control polygon, CIRCLE) from AP203/AP214 text files.
  • Non-destructive preview mesh and one-click clear.

Honest limitations

Not a general CAD package and not a CAM toolpath generator — it produces cut geometry and bend data; your cutting software still owns laser settings and toolpaths. K-factors are estimates. Bend a test coupon and measure it before production. macOS and Linux are untested; Windows with Blender 4.2.23 and 5.3.0 is verified.

Verified, not claimed. 236 automated checks, 43 artifact checks, a strict DXF audit and real smoke tests on two Blender versions. GPL-3.0-or-later — source ships with the extension.
