Blender add-on

Big model, small printer. Clean cuts.

SplitForge breaks an oversized mesh into build-volume-sized parts along natural joints, caps every cut, adds connectors, and checks the assembly fits before you export.

What it does

  • Shape-aware planning: oriented cut planes that cross the least geometry, with a vertex-density fallback.
  • Material-boundary and crease detection for natural joint planes.
  • Draw an editable seam loop; the cut follows the drawn 3D curve, not a fitted plane.
  • Watertight, capped parts with peg/socket connectors.
  • Verify Assembly Fit — checks connector alignment before export.
  • Deterministic planning: the same mesh and settings always produce the same cuts.
Performance. A 20k-vertex mesh plans in ~0.06 s thanks to a deterministic 4,000-point subsample. Full verification suite included.
