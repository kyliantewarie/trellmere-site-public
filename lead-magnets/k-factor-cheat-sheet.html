K-factor & bend allowance

One page. Print it, pin it by the brake.

The formulas

θ in radians · t thickness · R inside radius · K K-factor
BA = θ · (R + K·t)
OSSB = (R + t) · tan(θ / 2)
BD = 2 · OSSB − BA

Flat length from outside dimensions: FL = L1 + L2 − BD. Flat length by segments: sum straight lengths plus each BA.

Starting K-factors (air bending)

MaterialTypical KNotes
Mild steel0.40 – 0.44Start at 0.42
Stainless steel0.44 – 0.47Larger springback
Aluminium0.33 – 0.40Softer, more stretch

R > 2t → K trends to 0.5. R < t → K trends lower. These are estimates, not shop calibration.

The 10-minute coupon test

  1. Cut a 20 × 100 mm strip from the same sheet.
  2. Bend 90° on the same brake and tooling you'll use for the part.
  3. Measure outside legs L1 and L2 to 0.1 mm.
  4. Compute BD = 100 − (L1 + L2).
  5. Recover BA = 2·OSSB − BD.
  6. Solve K = (BA/θ − R) / t. Record it.
