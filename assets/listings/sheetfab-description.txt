**Stop round-tripping your sheet-metal parts through a second CAD program.**

SheetFab is a Blender extension that turns a folded sheet-metal part into a manufacturing-ready flat pattern — with the bend math done properly, inside the app you already model in.

---

### What it does

- **Build parts in the panel.** Material preset, thickness, inside radius, K-factor, leg lengths, bend angles, widths and holes. No JSON required (but power users can still use it).
- **Real bend math.** Bend allowance, bend deduction and outside-setback calculated from thickness, inside radius and K-factor — not a visual approximation.
- **See the flat blank.** Cut outline, bend lines with UP/DOWN direction, and hole circles, all in one preview.
- **Export clean manufacturing files.** Layered DXF (`CUT`, `BEND_UP`, `BEND_DOWN`, `HOLES`, `INFO`), a CSV bend table you can check against your shop, and an SVG preview.
- **Nest and compensate kerf.** Shape-aware nesting places copies on a sheet; kerf compensation offsets the cut path by half your tool diameter.
- **Import 2D STEP geometry.** POLYLINE, LINE, B_SPLINE control polygons and CIRCLEs from AP203/AP214 text files.

---

### Who it's for

Sheet-metal fabricators, product designers and makers who model in Blender and currently export to another CAD tool just to get a flat pattern for the laser or press brake.

### What it is not

- Not a general CAD package.
- Not a CAM toolpath generator — your cutting software still owns laser settings and toolpaths.
- Not a substitute for calibrating your own K-factor. **Bend a test coupon and measure it before production.**
