**Your AI-generated model looks great. It will not fit on your printer — and its arms, armour and props are fused into one solid shell.**

SplitForge is a Blender extension that turns that mesh into a set of printable parts in one panel. No cloud, no licence server, no round-trip through another tool.

---

### How it works

1. **Analyse** the model against your build volume (X/Y/Z + clearance).
2. **Plan shape-aware cuts.** The planner looks for planes that cross the least geometry — a narrow neck — instead of blindly bisecting the bounding box.
3. **Split** into capped, watertight parts that each fit the printer. The original mesh is untouched.
4. **Join them back** with fitted pegs and sockets, dowel holes or magnet pockets.
5. **Hollow** each part with a real millimetre wall and drill a drain hole at the lowest point.
6. **Check the fit** with an exploded view, lay every part flat, and export one STL per part.

Designed for miniatures, busts, cosplay props, terrain, scans and meshes from Meshy / Tripo / Rodin / Hunyuan3D.

### Honest limitations

- Cut smoothness depends on mesh density — a coarse mesh gives a stair-stepped edge.
- Connectors attach to flat cut faces; very thin or curved faces can be skipped. Check the **Verify Assembly** report before export.
- Hollowing needs a watertight part; the Analyse step warns you if the mesh is not watertight.
- STL export only — no 3MF plate packing, slicer integration or automatic supports.
- Material presets give starting clearances, not a calibrated profile for your printer. Print a test coupon first.
